## 💬 Dialogue (對話)

### Part 1

**A：** 最近你好似有啲煩喎，成個人都有啲悶悶不樂咁，係咪喺人際關係方面遇到啲乜嘢問題啊？

> *Recently you seem a bit troubled, your whole person seems a bit unhappy. Have you encountered some kind of problem in interpersonal relationships?*

**B：** 係啊，最近同啲同事相處嗰陣，間中都會覺得有啲壓力，都唔係好知道點樣先可以拿捏好分寸，驚講多錯多。

> *Yes, recently when getting along with colleagues, I sometimes feel some pressure. I don't really know how to judge the appropriate limits — afraid that the more I speak, the more mistakes I'll make.*

**A：** 咁係邊一方面令到你覺得最困擾呢？係溝通嘅問題定係大家做嘢方式唔同嘅問題？

> *So which aspect troubles you the most? Is it a communication problem, or a problem of everyone having different ways of doing things?*

**B：** 主要係有啲同事講嘢比較直接，有時語氣比較衝，我就會喺度諗，佢哋係咪針對緊我呢，搞到自己心入面有啲唔舒服，甚至會亂咁諗嘢。

> *Mainly some colleagues speak quite directly, sometimes their tone is rather blunt. I would wonder if they're targeting me, making myself feel uncomfortable inside, even thinking all kinds of things.*

**A：** 明白，有時語氣真係好容易令人誤會。咁你之後係點樣處理呢啲情況？有冇試過同佢哋溝通下啊？

> *I understand. Sometimes tone of voice can really easily cause misunderstandings. So how did you handle these situations afterward? Have you tried communicating with them?*

---

### Part 2

**B：** 首先我會試下先令自己冷靜落嚟，唔好即刻有情緒反應，然後試下企喺對方嘅立場諗一諗。後來發現，其實好多時候都只係因為工作需要，講嘢直接啲，未必真係有惡意。

> *First I would try to calm myself down, not react emotionally right away, then try to stand in the other person's position and think about it. Later I discovered that actually most of the time it's just because of work needs — speaking more directly doesn't necessarily mean ill will.*

**A：** 咁你咁樣諗完之後，個人有冇覺得輕鬆咗啲？

> *So after thinking this way, did you feel more relaxed?*

**B：** 有啊，真係輕鬆咗唔少，冇之前咁容易鑽牛角尖。我亦都開始主動同同事溝通，多啲表達自己嘅諗法同感受，希望可以避免誤會越積越多。

> *Yes, I really feel much more relaxed, not as prone to overthinking as before. I also started proactively communicating with colleagues, expressing my thoughts and feelings more, hoping to avoid misunderstandings from accumulating.*

**A：** 咁樣做好好喎，溝通真係好重要。咁你自己覺得，建立良好人際關係最重要係乜嘢呢？

> *That's great, communication is really important. So what do you think is the most important thing in building good interpersonal relationships?*

**B：** 我覺得最重要係尊重同埋理解，大家都要試下企喺對方嘅角度諗下。另外都要保持真誠，唔好一味迎合或者討好人，咁樣反而唔長久。

> *I think the most important things are respect and understanding. Everyone needs to try to think from the other person's perspective. Also need to maintain sincerity — don't just cater to or flatter people, because that way won't last long anyway.*

**A：** 講得好有道理。咁如果喺關係入面真係遇到矛盾或者衝突，你通常會點做？

> *That makes a lot of sense. So if you really encounter contradictions or conflicts in a relationship, what would you usually do?*

---

### Part 3

**B：** 我覺得逃避只會令到問題越嚟越大，積埋積埋就更加難解決。不如適當咁溝通，大家坦白啲講出自己嘅諗法，再互相包容同體諒，反而可以令關係變得更加穩固。

> *I think avoiding the issue will only make the problem bigger and bigger. Accumulating them makes it even harder to solve. Better to communicate appropriately, everyone speak frankly about their thoughts, then mutually包容 and empathize — this can actually make the relationship become more stable.*

**A：** 聽你咁講，人際關係真係一門學問嚟㗎，需要不斷咁學習同埋調整。

> *Hearing you say that, interpersonal relationships really are a field of study — requiring constant learning and adjustment.*

**B：** 冇錯，真係要慢慢累積經驗，先會做得越嚟越好。

> *That's right. You really need to slowly accumulate experience before you can do better and better.*

## 🔑 Key Grammar Points

### 1. 有啲...咁 (seems a bit...)

Used to describe someone's state or appearance.

> **Pattern:** 有啲 + adjective + 咁
> 
> **Example:** 成個人都有啲悶悶不樂**咁** — the whole person seems a bit unhappy

### 2. 係咪...啊？ (Is it that...?)

Question pattern for confirming something.

> **Pattern:** 係咪 + statement + 啊？
> 
> **Example:** **係咪**喺人際關係方面遇到啲乜嘢問題**啊**？ — Have you encountered some problem in relationships?

### 3. 點樣先可以... (how to be able to...)

Expresses how to achieve something.

> **Pattern:** 點樣 + 先可以 + verb
> 
> **Example:** 唔係好知道**點樣先可以**拿捏好分寸 — don't really know how to judge the appropriate limits

### 4. 驚... (afraid that...)

Expresses worry or fear about something.

> **Pattern:** 驚 + clause
> 
> **Example:** **驚**講多錯多 — afraid that the more I speak, the more mistakes I'll make

### 5. 係咪...緊我 (are they...ing me?)

Progressive aspect with 緊 to indicate ongoing action.

> **Pattern:** 係咪 + verb + 緊 + object
> 
> **Example:** 佢哋**係咪針對緊我**呢 — are they targeting me?

### 6. 搞到... (making it so that...)

Indicates causing a result.

> **Pattern:** 搞到 + result clause
> 
> **Example:** **搞到**自己心入面有啲唔舒服 — making myself feel uncomfortable inside

### 7. 有冇試過...？ (Have you ever tried...?)

Asking about past experience.

> **Pattern:** 有冇試過 + verb + 下 + 啊？
> 
> **Example:** **有冇試過**同佢哋溝通**下啊**？ — Have you tried communicating with them?

### 8. 企喺...立場/角度 (stand in someone's position/perspective)

Metaphorical expression for empathy.

> **Pattern:** 企喺 + 對方嘅 + 立場/角度 + 諗
> 
> **Example:** 試下**企喺對方嘅立場**諗一諗 — try to stand in the other person's position and think

### 9. 未必 (not necessarily)

Expresses uncertainty or negation of certainty.

> **Example:** 講嘢直接啲，**未必**真係有惡意 — speaking directly doesn't necessarily mean ill will

### 10. 越...越... (the more... the more...)

Correlative construction showing proportional change.

> **Pattern:** 越 + verb/adj + 越 + verb/adj
> 
> **Example:** 誤會**越**積**越**多 — misunderstandings accumulate more and more

### 11. 一味 (blindly, just keep doing)

Indicates doing something without thinking.

> **Pattern:** 一味 + verb
> 
> **Example:** 唔好**一味迎合**或者討好人 — don't just blindly cater to or flatter people

### 12. 只會令到... (will only make...)

Expresses a negative consequence.

> **Pattern:** 只會令到 + result
> 
> **Example:** 逃避**只會令到**問題越嚟越大 — avoiding will only make the problem bigger

### 13. 不如... (might as well / better to...)

Suggestion pattern.

> **Pattern:** 不如 + suggestion
> 
> **Example:** **不如**適當咁溝通 — better to communicate appropriately

### 14. 先會... (only then will...)

Indicates a condition must be met first.

> **Pattern:** 先會 + result
> 
> **Example:** 慢慢累積經驗，**先會**做得越嚟越好 — slowly accumulate experience, only then will you do better and better

## ❓ Comprehension Questions (回答問題)

### Questions & Answers

**1. A 覺得 B 最近點樣？**
> *What does A think B has been like recently?*
>
> **答：** A 覺得 B 最近有啲煩，成個人都有啲悶悶不樂。
> *(A thinks B seems troubled and unhappy recently.)*

**2. B 覺得壓力主要嚟自邊度？**
> *Where does B feel the pressure mainly comes from?*
>
> **答：** 壓力主要嚟自同同事相處嗰陣。
> *(The pressure mainly comes from getting along with colleagues.)*

**3. B 點解會覺得唔舒服？**
> *Why does B feel uncomfortable?*
>
> **答：** 因為有啲同事講嘢比較直接，語氣比較衝，B 會以為佢哋針對緊自己。
> *(Because some colleagues speak directly with a blunt tone, B thought they were targeting him/her.)*

**4. B 一開始點樣理解同事嘅說話？**
> *How did B initially understand the colleagues' words?*
>
> **答：** B 一開始以為同事針對緊自己，搞到心入面唔舒服，甚至亂咁諗嘢。
> *(B initially thought colleagues were targeting him/her, feeling uncomfortable and overthinking.)*

**5. 之後 B 點樣處理自己嘅情緒？**
> *How did B handle his/her emotions afterward?*
>
> **答：** B 會先令自己冷靜落嚟，唔好即刻有情緒反應，然後企喺對方立場諗一諗。
> *(B would first calm down, not react emotionally, then try to think from the other person's perspective.)*

**6. B 點樣改變自己嘅諗法？**
> *How did B change his/her thinking?*
>
> **答：** B 發現同事講嘢直接係因為工作需要，未必有惡意。
> *(B discovered that colleagues speak directly because of work needs, not necessarily with ill will.)*

**7. B 發現同事講嘢直接嘅原因係乜嘢？**
> *What reason did B discover for colleagues speaking directly?*
>
> **答：** 因為工作需要。
> *(Because of work needs.)*

**8. B 後來有乜嘢改變？**
> *What changes did B make later?*
>
> **答：** B 開始主動同同事溝通，多啲表達自己嘅諗法同感受。
> *(B started proactively communicating with colleagues, expressing thoughts and feelings more.)*

**9. B 認為良好人際關係最重要係乜嘢？**
> *What does B think is most important for good interpersonal relationships?*
>
> **答：** 最重要係尊重同埋理解，要企喺對方角度諗，同埋保持真誠，唔好一味迎合討好人。
> *(Most important are respect and understanding, thinking from others' perspectives, and maintaining sincerity — not blindly catering to people.)*

**10. 遇到矛盾時，B 建議點樣做？**
> *When encountering conflicts, what does B suggest doing?*
>
> **答：** 唔好逃避，要適當咁溝通，坦白講出諗法，互相包容同體諒。
> *(Don't avoid — communicate appropriately, speak frankly, and mutually包容 and empathize.)*

## 💡 Useful Expressions for Daily Life

### Expressing Concern
- 你最近點啊？ — How have you been recently?
- 你好似有啲煩喎 — You seem a bit troubled
- 係咪遇到啲乜嘢問題啊？ — Have you encountered some problem?

### Giving Advice
- 首先...然後... — First... then...
- 不如... — Might as well / Better to...
- 試下... — Try to...

### Sharing Feelings
- 我覺得... — I feel/think that...
- 心入面有啲唔舒服 — Feel uncomfortable inside
- 輕鬆咗唔少 — Feel much more relaxed

### Building Relationships
- 尊重同埋理解 — Respect and understanding
- 保持真誠 — Maintain sincerity
- 互相包容同體諒 — Mutually包容 and empathize
- 企喺對方嘅角度諗 — Think from the other person's perspective
